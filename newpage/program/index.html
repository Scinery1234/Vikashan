Who it’s for

For anyone at a turning point in their career

  • You’re in a job that doesn’t fit any more, and you’re not sure what would.
  • You know what you want to do next, but you can’t seem to get started.
  • You’re between jobs, coming back after a break, or changing careers.
  • You’re tired or burnt out, and need to steady yourself before deciding anything.
How it works

Four weeks, one step at a time

  1. Take the quiz. Three minutes to see where you’re at right now, and a 4-week plan to match.
  2. Week 1: start your plan. Your plan is already loaded into New Page. Your Career Coach helps you take the first steps.
  3. Weeks 2 and 3: your 1:1 with Vik. A 60-minute online session. Vik has seen your plan and progress beforehand, so you start where you are, not from scratch.
  4. Week 4: check in and choose what’s next. Retake the quiz, see how far you’ve moved, and decide your next steps.

Questions

Common questions

Is this therapy?

No. New Page is coaching. It doesn’t replace a counsellor, doctor or other health professional. If you’re going through a hard time, Lifeline is there on 13 11 14, any time.

How much time does it take?

Most steps take 15 to 30 minutes, and your plan is paced to your situation. A couple of hours a week is plenty.

Are the coaches AI?

Yes, except Vik. The Career Coach and specialist coaches are AI, built for this program. Your 1:1 session is with Vik in person, online.

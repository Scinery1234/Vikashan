How it works

Three steps

  1. Tell Sky about you. A 2-minute check-in, your resume if you have one, and a few optional questions about how you like to work. Sky keeps learning as you talk.
  2. Work on what matters. Plan your week, work out your direction with the Compass, tailor a resume to a job, practise an interview out loud.
  3. Come back whenever. Sky remembers your goals and what you decided. Pick up where you left off, from any device.

Questions

Common questions

Is Sky a real person?

No. Sky is an AI, built for New Page. It says so, and it never pretends otherwise. If you want a human coach, the four-week program includes a 1:1 session with one.

Is this therapy?

No. It’s coaching. It doesn’t replace a counsellor, doctor or other health professional. If you’re going through a hard time, Lifeline is on 13 11 14, any time.

What does Sky remember, and who can see it?

Sky keeps short notes on your goals, strengths, limits and decisions, plus your resume and your answers about yourself. It’s all encrypted with a key just for you. You can see, edit and delete it in the app. Nobody else can read your chats unless you choose to share them.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access to the end of the month you’ve paid for. Sky’s memory of you is kept for 30 days after that, in case you come back, then moved to the Trash and deleted after 12 months. You can download or delete it yourself at any time.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The demo gives you a day in the app as a sample member, no sign-up.

Who is behind it?

Purposefields, a vocational wellbeing organisation for purpose-driven people. We believe career development and wellbeing should be for everybody, regardless of money. That’s why Sky and the tools cost $25, and why scholarships exist for the full program.
